# Contributing to Triad

Thanks for contributing. Before opening a PR:

- Read [Attributing Your Changes](https://github.com/Triad-Sector/Triad_Sector/wiki/Attributing-Your-Changes): how to license and mark your work so it survives review and future upstream merges.
- Follow the Space Wizards' [codebase conventions](https://docs.spacestation14.com/en/general-development/codebase-info/codebase-organization.html) where Triad doesn't say otherwise.
- Get in [our Discord](https://discord.gg/J9Rn76nKbz) to coordinate bigger changes before you build them, and don't be afraid to ask for help.

## AI-generated content

AI assistance is permitted only if you are already proficient in the domain and could have produced the work yourself: use it to enhance your existing workflow, not to generate content you don't understand. PRs that read as AI-generated without demonstrated understanding may be closed at maintainer discretion.

## Changelogs

Player-facing changes get a changelog entry: fill in the `:cl:` block in the PR template with `add` / `remove` / `tweak` / `fix` lines written for players, and delete the block entirely for PRs players will never notice.
